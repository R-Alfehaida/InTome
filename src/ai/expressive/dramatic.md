### 8. Dramatic reading

This book is being read with more drama than usual. Sections 3 to 7 still apply, and the styles and tags are still only those listed, but:

* Also give quoted speech a style when its own words plainly carry one of the emotions in section 5, even with a plain "said" or no speech tag at all. An exclamation of delight is `excited`, an accusation is `raised voice, angry`, a plea in danger is `frightened`, a reply full of mockery is `sarcastic`. Lines that are calm, neutral or unclear still have no style.
* Add a vocal tag wherever the scene clearly implies a sound, not only where the text names it, such as a `<gasp>` at a shock or a `<sigh>` before a weary reply. Four or five per page is still the most.
